## Park City  Hotel Peaks Hotel Booking Analysis
## Overview
This project analyzes hotel booking data to evaluate reservation trends, guest behavior, seasonal demand, and cancellation patterns using Excel and Tableau.
## Business Problem
The project aimed to uncover factors affecting booking performance, guest retention, food options and cancellations to support operational and customer experience improvements.
## Tools Used
- Excel.
- Tableau.
## Skills Demonstrated
- KPI Reporting.
- Trend Analysis.
- Cancellation Analysis.
- Data Viaualization.
- Customer Behavior Analysis.
## Dashboard Features
- Booking Trends.
- Cancellation Analysis.
- Seasonal Demand Tracking.
- Guest Retention Metrics.
## Key Insights
- New guests accounted for the majority of hotel bookings (96.81%), while returning guest represented a very small percentage, indicating low customer retention and returning booking rates.
- Booking activity and cancellations flunctuated across different months, with peak bookings periods also experiencing higher cancellation volumes, suggesting seasonal deamnd patterns and operational pressure during busy periods.
- Bed & Breakfast was the most preferred meal option among guests, significantly outperforming other meal categories, while Full Board recorded the lowest customer preference.
- Bookings were dominated by adult guests, while bookings involving children remained significantly lower, suggesting that most reservations were made by small family groups or adult travelers.
  ## Recommendations
 - Introduce loyalty programs and personalized offers for returning guests to improve custonmer retention and increase repeat bookings.
 - Implement proactive booking confirmation and flexible reservation strategies during peak periods to help reduce cancellation rates and improve occupancy management.
 - Develop family-focused packages and promotional offers targeting travelers with children to increase family bookings.
 - Expand and promote high-demand meal packages such as Bed & Breakfast while reassessing underperforming meal plans based on customer preferences and booking behavior.
